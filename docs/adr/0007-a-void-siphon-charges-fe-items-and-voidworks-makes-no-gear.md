# A Void Siphon charges FE items, and Voidworks makes no gear

The spec planned void gear of Voidworks' own, spending motes from a Void Siphon at the player's
feet, and the glossary said void energy is not FE. Nothing of it was built. Gearworks now owns a
suit, Gear, that runs on FE and takes it from any mod's charger through NeoForge's item energy
capability.

**Decision.** Voidworks makes no gear. A Void Siphon spends its motes by the spend rule at the
player's feet, and the void energy that releases converts, at a fixed rate in the server config,
into FE in the FE items it charges: any item that takes FE through NeoForge's item energy
capability, Gearworks' or another mod's. Void energy is still never stored, and motes are still all
Voidworks stores; the FE is the other mod's to keep. Voidworks talks only to NeoForge, so it
requires nothing more than it did.

## Considered options

- **Void gear of our own**, as planned: gear that could make Void Pressure its mechanic, such as
  working better in the Nether, but a second suit beside Gearworks', its stats and upgrades to build
  and balance, and none of the FE world reaching it.

## Consequences

- Where a Siphon charges still matters: the same motes give the most FE in the Nether, so charging
  is one more reason to carry motes downhill.
- Once it is FE, Voidworks has no say in where it goes: an FE item that empties into a base's
  machines makes motes power them, by the rate.
