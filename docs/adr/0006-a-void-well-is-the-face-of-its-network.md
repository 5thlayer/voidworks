# A Void Well is the face of its network

A Void Well's core holds a large store of motes, but its network's Voidstone holds motes too, and
conduction keeps the two apart. Seen in game: Jade showed a reservoir's network holding 123 motes
while its core held none, because motes between Voidstone settle in a staircase, each one more than
its neighbour nearer the Well, and nothing pulls them in. A dragon, which loaded only from the core,
found nothing to take. And a Well's network pressure, its fill to the eighth power, stays below even
one mote in a Voidstone until the Well is about 71% full, so below that it gave nothing back to
machines elsewhere on its network. What a player read of a Well was not what a dragon or a machine
could use.

**Decision.** Anything that asks about or uses a Well counts its whole network, its core and every
Voidstone together: what it holds, how full it is, its fill target, as a share of the network's
whole capacity, what a dragon loads, at once and from anywhere on it, the core first, and what
heals a dragon. The core keeps its store: an unload goes into it, and only once it is full into the
Voidstone nearest the Well. A spender pulling motes toward it draws them out of a Well whatever the
Well's pressure, so a Well still soaks up its network's surplus and gives it back to whatever draws:
only the grades worth something where the spender spends them, and while it draws, the Well soaks
up none of those on their way to it.
Several Wells on one network are one stop, a dragon never carrying between them; they may be
different owners', who then share the network's motes, each owner's dragons judging it by that
owner's targets alone and counting every dragon's motes already on their way to it.

## Considered options

- **A store**, as it was, with its flows fixed: what is in a Well and what is in its network would
  stay two numbers, one shown and the other used.
- **A station** that holds nothing and docks dragons on its network's Voidstone: what is read is
  what is used, but at 16 motes a Voidstone a dragon's load of thousands needs hundreds of free
  Voidstone, and a Well would add no storage.
- **One Well per network**: networks merge whenever Voidstone touches, by a player's block or a
  Displacer's harvest, so it would take refusing the merge, a Well sealing its network, or a Well
  going still; each breaks the spec's "networks that touch merge, harmlessly".

## Consequences

- A second Well on a network adds its core's capacity and another place to stop; whether that is
  worth its build and its price is its owner's call.
- A network is open to whoever joins it: another owner's Well on it shares its motes, as another's
  machine on it already does. Keeping others out is claiming the land, not the Well's job.
